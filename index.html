Before you add a key: your messages (and your voice, if you use Accurate voice input) are sent to Google or OpenAI using the key you enter below, under their own privacy policies — not stored by Jarvis anywhere except this browser.

Why I need this: this key is what lets me actually think and talk — it connects me to Google's Gemini AI, which powers my answers, translations, and everything else I do. Without one, I cannot respond at all.

Don't have one yet? It's free:
1. Open aistudio.google.com/app/apikey
2. Sign in with any Google account
3. Tap "Create API key" (choose or create a project if asked)
4. Copy the key it gives you, then paste it above and tap Save
